Aaj ki headlines mein humne dekha ki **'Rs 1.5 Lakh Crore ke MTF problem ke karan Smallcap stocks mein bada sell-off ho raha hai.'** Toh aakhir yeh **Margin Trading Facility (MTF)** hota kya hai aur yeh retail investors ke portfolio ko kaise crash kar sakta hai? Aaiye aasan bhasha mein samjhte hain. **1. MTF Kya Hai?** MTF yaani Margin Trading Facility ek aisa tool hai jisse brokers aapko aapke paas maujood capital se 3 se 4 guna zyada ki shares khareedne ki permission dete hain. Agar aapke paas Rs 1 lakh hain, toh MTF ke zariye aap Rs 4 lakh tak ke smallcap ya midcap shares khareed sakte hain. Baaki ka Rs 3 lakh broker aapko funding ke roop mein deta hai jispar interest charge hota hai. **2. Leverage Ka Double-Edged Sword Effect:** Jab market upar jaata hai, toh MTF aapke profits ko multiply kar deta hai. Lekin jab market neeche aata hai, toh nuksan bhi multiply hota hai. **3. MTF Crisis Kaise Shuru Hota Hai?** Maan lijiye aapne kisi Smallcap stock mein MTF ke zariye leveraged position li. Jab woh stock 10% ya 15% girta hai, toh broker aapko **'Margin Call'** bhejta hai. Broker kehta hai: *"Yatoh aur cash account mein dalo, nahi toh hum aapke shares market mein force sell kar denge."* Jab hazaron retail investors ek sath margin call fill nahi kar paate, toh brokers unke shares ko open market mein dump kar dete hain. Iss forced selling ki wajah se stock 20% ya 30% aur gir jata hai. Isi chain-reaction ko **'Leveraged Unwinding Cascade'** kehte hain. **Lesson for Investors:** Smallcap bull run ke dauran kabhi bhi over-leveraged ho kar MTF trading na karein. Jab liquidity sukhti hai, toh MTF sabse pehle smallcap stocks ko crash karta hai. Trading ke liye hamesha utna hi capital use karein jitna aap risk kar sakte hain, aur risk management rules ko follow karein!" --- ### [SECTION 14: CONCLUSION] **[Visual: Host on screen with summarizing points, call to action for subscriber engagement.]** **Host:** "Toh dosto, yeh tha 26 October 2026 ka poora market update.
