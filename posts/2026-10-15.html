Educational Market Lesson: AT-1 Bonds & SC Ruling *(20:30 - 22:30)* **[Visual Cue: Educational slide explaining "What are AT-1 Bonds?" showing features: Perpetual Nature, High Interest, Loss Absorbency Clause, Supreme Court Impact.]** **Host:** "Aaiye ab aate hain hamare sabse valuable segment par—**Today's Educational Market Lesson**. Aaj Yes Bank ka share 10% se 20% crash hua kyunki Supreme Court ne ₹8,415 crore ke AT-1 bonds ko write-off karne ke faisle ko radd kar diya. Lekin **AT-1 Bonds** hote kya hain aur court ke is faisle ka kya matlab hai? Aaiye aasan bhasha me samajhte hain. **AT-1 Bonds (Additional Tier-1 Bonds) kya hote hain?** AT-1 bonds ek tarah ke un-secured, perpetual bonds hote hain jo banks apna capital base badhane ke liye issue karte hain. Inke 3 sabse bade features hote hain: 1. **No Maturity Date (Perpetual):** Inki koi fixed maturity date nahi hoti. Bank chahe to kuch saalon baad inhe call option ke zariye wapas khareed sakta hai, par aap bank se apna paisa maang nahi sakte. 2. **High Interest Rate:** Kyunki ye risky hote hain, isliye bank inpar normal FD se zyada interest (coupon rate) deta hai. 3. **Loss Absorbency Clause:** Agar bank ki financial sthiti kharab hoti hai ya bank doobne lagta hai, to RBI aur bank ke paas yeh power hoti hai ki wo in bonds ki value ko ZERO (Write-off) kar dein ya inhe equity me badal dein. **Yes Bank Case me kya hua?** Jab 2020 me Yes Bank ka restructuring plan aaya, tab RBI aur administrator ne Rs 8,415 crore ke AT-1 bonds ko poori tarah write-off (zero) kar diya tha. Bondholders (jisme retail investors aur pension funds bhi the) High Court gaye. High Court ne is write-off ko cancel kar diya. Aaj Supreme Court ne bhi High Court ke order ko sahi thehraaya. Iska matlab yeh hai ki Yes Bank ko ab in bondholders ka paisa ya to waapas karna pad sakta hai ya unhe compensation dena pad sakta hai. Is vajah se bank ki balance sheet par ek bada liability ka bhojh aa sakta hai—aur yahi vajah hai ki aaj Yes Bank ka share crash hua. **Investors ke liye Lesson:** Kabhi bhi sirf 'High Interest' ya 'High Return' dekh kar AT-1 bonds jaise complex financial instruments me paisa na lagayein. Risk aur capital structure me wo bond kahan stand karta hai, yeh samajhna bohot zaroori hai!" --- ## 14.
